# Project Statement: GiveLoop

## Problem
Many people have perfectly usable items—clothes, furniture, textbooks, laptops—that they no longer need but hesitate to throw away due to environmental concerns or because they know someone else could use them. Conversely, students and lower-income individuals often struggle to afford these basic necessities.

## Objective
To build "GiveLoop," a free, community-driven giveaway and donation platform that connects donors with individuals in need, facilitating the circular economy, reducing waste, and fostering community support.

## Target Users
- **Donors:** Individuals (primarily students/residents) looking to responsibly declutter.
- **Recipients:** Students, individuals, or families seeking affordable access to household items, educational materials, and electronics.

## Scope
- Web-based platform for listing, browsing, and requesting items.
- Focus on local communities (defaulting to Bengaluru), with the capability to scale to other cities.
- Secure request/approval flow to ensure donations go to genuine recipients.
